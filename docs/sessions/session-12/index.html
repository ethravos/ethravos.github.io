    Recap

    In our last session, you had met up with Whisper and gotten quite a bit of useful information on the Emerald Senate and Hollow Keys from him, piecing together some of the mysterious things that have been occuring recently.

    In addition to that, you had managed to convince him to join your party for the time-being in order to go off-grid at the Ashwood Dryads. During your travels, you had encountered some friction with some rough terrain and a very mysterious pop-up circus called Khalif's Velvet Waggon in the middle of the Feylume Forest. You dodged the very charasmatic hype-man for the circus and moved forward on your journey.

    Finally, while making camp, Whisper taught Pennyglass more about survival in the wild and the begginnings of what it is to be a Ranger.

    The Session

    After making your way East to the grove, the party arrives to the opening the grove exists within.

    Suddenly, each of you receive scarring visions, including Whisper. Whisper freezes, staring at the hill with a look of horror.

    Visions

    Verdant sees a vision of the entire druid encampment lit ablaze. A family stumbling out of the front gates on fire, falling to the ground in agony. The vision fades.

    Pennyglass has a vision of Whisper kneeling on the ground, hunched over and holding his stomach, possibly weeping. The vision fades.

    Aurora looks up at the hill and sees it completely flattened and a crater smoking at the center. Over the crater, a white female phantasmal silhouette hovers over the crater. In an instant the being's face was inches from hers. The vision fades.

    The Tuneleaf

    After all of these horrifying visions, you all decided that it would be better to bring Whisper to Aurora's mother's home to stay for the timebeing. You warned The Warden in the grove of these visions, and the group decided to head to a nearby "holy" place, the Tuneleaf, at Aurora's request.

    While at this pond, Aurora communed with whatever being has been reaching out to her. She had another vision of this being of light and she spoke...

    " Fear the Emerald Petal. "

    Then again, seemingly in response to Aurora's questions...

    " Walk with grace, determination, and fear... "

    Then, finally...

    " Never leave a whisper in the dark "

    And the communion shattered.

    The Whisper Follows

    Aurora relayed this interaction to the group and you all decided it would be better to bring whisper with you to Goldrum Sol with you on your search for why Aliath had visited the library there. Whisper agreed and so you all headed back to the mysterious Hollow Key door in the Feylume Forest.

    With some amount of turbulance, you manage to find the door to Goldrum Sol. Getting through, Whisper takes the lead and brings you all to the ground-level of the city with ease and familiarity.

    Finally, you made your way to the library in the city and, though he was hesitant, Whisper follows...
