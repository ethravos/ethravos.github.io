The Trivis Orchid is a rare flower that grows in very specific environments within the Feywild. It seems to grow only on the sides of floating rock clusters within the "Erinfall Orchard". This is considered something close to a "holy place" by those who live in the feywild (though fey don't necessarily have holiness as a concept).
